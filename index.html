Retain-AI — Customer Retention Decision Platform

Churn prediction · explainability

Predicts which customers will churn, explains why with SHAP, quantifies revenue at risk, and recommends the highest-value interventions.

Built around a fictional B2B SaaS company (~20,000 customers, ₹-denominated contracts) with a Customer Success team that can only afford to proactively contact ~500 accounts a month. The question the platform answers isn't just "who might churn" — it's who to prioritise once revenue and intervention cost are on the table.

  • Engineered a synthetic, relational enterprise dataset — customers, contracts, subscriptions, product usage, support tickets, and payments — with a strict 180-day feature window and 90-day churn label to avoid data leakage.
  • Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost baselines on ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, calibration, and lift; the calibrated XGBoost model is the current front-runner.
  • Added SHAP explainability so every prediction comes with its top drivers — usage decline, support deterioration, renewal proximity — not just a probability.
  • Designed a business decision layer that ranks customers by net expected value (revenue at risk × intervention success rate − intervention cost), not churn probability alone.
  • Planning a Streamlit dashboard — executive overview, risk explorer, customer 360, and an LLM-generated retention brief per account — as the final decision-support layer.
